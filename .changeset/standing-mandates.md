Standing mandates (spec 150): `createStandingMandates` gives the center's side — `grant` takes a
person's token once, as her consent to let one agent act for her while she is away; `token` asks,
at each use, for the agent's mandate of now (her claims read again, ten minutes); `revoke`
withdraws it.
